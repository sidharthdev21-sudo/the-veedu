# Aalayam Marketing Plan · Storyboards

Three sets of storyboards, all built from `marketing-plan.md`:

- **A. The plan as an explainer video** (Episode 8): one scene per part of the plan.
- **B. Campaign content storyboards**: shot-by-shot plans for the Reels, Stories, creator posts and pop-up that execute the plan.
- **C. The customer journey storyboard**: "Anu's" path through AIDAS, showing which asset meets her at each step.

House style for everything: dark palette for the explainer (Episode 1–7 identity); **real L–3XL women, natural daylight, honest captions** for campaign content. No customer names, phone numbers or order details on screen. Anything that is an example is labelled as an example.

## B. Campaign content storyboards

Each storyboard shows: **mission** (7Ms), **AIDAS stage**, **format**, frames, **CTA** and the **metric** that judges it.

### B1 · Reel: "Same set, L to 3XL" (hero awareness Reel)

**Mission:** Awareness · **Stage:** Attention → Interest · **Format:** 9:16 Reel, 15–20 s · **Test:** user-as-hero (this) vs product-as-hero (flat-lay)

| Frame | Time | Shot | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2 s | Close-up: hands holding the folded co-ord set, soft daylight | "One set." | Soft beat starts |
| 2 | 2–6 s | Four women (L, XL, 2XL, 3XL) step into frame one by one, same set | Size label on each: "L · XL · 2XL · 3XL" | Beat |
| 3 | 6–10 s | Each turns, sits, walks: the set moves with them | "Fits every one of us." | |
| 4 | 10–14 s | Close-up: fabric texture, pocket, waistband | "60×60 cotton · pockets · relaxed fit" | |
| 5 | 14–18 s | All four smile together; wordmark | **"Fits the first time."** · "Sizes L–3XL · link in bio" | Beat resolves |

**CTA:** link in bio (UTM `ig_reel_sameset`) · **Metric:** reach, saves rate, profile visits · **Note:** models must genuinely wear that size (the authenticity test).

### B2 · Reel: "How we measure every garment"

**Mission:** Awareness → Trial · **Stage:** Interest → Desire · **Format:** Reel, 20–25 s, product-as-hero

| Frame | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measuring tape snaps open on a garment on a table | "Why we measure every piece we sell" |
| 2 | Tape across the bust of the top, number appears | "Bust: shown on every product page" |
| 3 | Tape down the pant length | "Length: so you know before you buy" |
| 4 | Phone screen: product page with the measurement table | "Check the numbers → pick your size" |
| 5 | WhatsApp chat icon | "Still unsure? Send us your measurements." |

**CTA:** "WhatsApp us your size" · **Metric:** saves, link clicks, fit checks started.

### B3 · Story sequence: "WhatsApp fit check" (conversion)

**Mission:** Trial · **Stage:** Desire → Action · **Format:** 4 Stories

| Story | Content | Interaction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "XL top, 2XL bottom? You're not alone." | Poll: "Is that you?" Yes / Sometimes |
| 2 | "Send us 3 numbers: bust, waist, hip" | Tutorial graphic (from the size guide) |
| 3 | Example chat (labelled **example**): "Bust 42 · Waist 38 · Hip 46" → "We suggest XL in the top, 2XL in the pant" | — |
| 4 | "We'll tell you honestly, even if a style won't suit you." | **Link sticker → WhatsApp** (UTM) |

**Metric:** sticker taps, fit checks, fit-check-to-order rate.

### B4 · Creator post brief (nano creators)

**Mission:** Awareness + Trial · **Format:** 1 Reel + 3 Stories per creator · **Payment:** flat fee + product; unique code

| Beat | What the creator shows | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unboxing in her real space | Her real size, said out loud ("I wear 2XL") |
| 2 | Try-on: sit, walk, lift arms | Honest fit comments, good and bad |
| 3 | Measurement check against the product page | "I checked the measurements first" |
| 4 | Outfit for her day (work, temple, coffee) | Her own styling |
| 5 | CTA | Her code + "WhatsApp them your size" |

**Never:** scripted superlatives, fake urgency, or asking her audience to "like and share for a reward". **Monitor:** engagement time pattern (watch for 1–2 AM spikes), clicks and orders per code.

### B5 · Pop-up: "Try-on day, Kochi"

**Mission:** Trial + owned audience · **Stage:** Desire → Action

| Moment | Scene | Capture |
|---|---|---|
| Before (T-7) | Teaser Reel: rail of co-ords in every size; **one clear date and time** | Waitlist sign-ups |
| Arrival | Welcome table: tape measure, size card, QR to WhatsApp | Opt-in to the broadcast list |
| Try-on | Mirrors, a fitting area, helpers suggesting sizes | Sizes tried vs bought (to the fit dataset, no names) |
| Purchase | Card or UPI; thank-you tote | Sales by style and size |
| After (T+1) | "Thank you, Kochi" Reel with consenting customers | Reach; Google review requests |

### B6 · Review loop (repeat)

**Mission:** Repeat · **Stage:** Satisfaction

| Day | Touchpoint | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Care card in the parcel | "Wash cold, dry in shade. Love the fit? Tell us." + QR |
| Day 7 | WhatsApp message | "How's the fit? Share a photo review with your size" |
| Day 10 | Story repost (with permission) | Customer photo + "She wears 2XL" |
| Day 30 | Fit-memory message | "We've saved your sizes. New styles in XL / 2XL are here." |
| Second order | Thank-you | A small reward (operant conditioning), no fake discount |

**Metric:** reviews per 10 orders, repeat rate, referrals.

## C. Customer journey storyboard: "Anu's path"

*Anu is an illustrative persona, not a real customer.*

| Panel | AIDAS stage | Scene | Aalayam asset | What we measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Attention | 10 PM, scrolling Instagram; a Reel shows four women in the same set, L to 3XL | B1 Reel / creator post | Reach, 3-second views |
| 2 | Interest | She taps the profile; the "Size guide" Highlight shows how to measure | Instagram Highlights, B2 | Profile visits, saves |
| 3 | Desire | On the product page she sees the measurement table and a review from a 2XL woman | Product page with measurements | Product views, add-to-cart |
| 4 | Action (doubt) | "XL top, 2XL pant?" She taps WhatsApp and sends three numbers | WhatsApp fit check (B3) | Fit checks started |
| 5 | Action | A reply within a few hours recommends sizes honestly; she orders | Checkout via UTM link | Conversion, fit-check-to-order |
| 6 | Satisfaction | Parcel arrives in 5–7 days; care card; it fits | Packaging, care card | Size-exchange rate |
| 7 | Satisfaction → loop | Day 7: she posts a photo review; a friend her size sees it | B6 review loop | Reviews, referrals |
| 8 | Repeat | Day 30: "New styles in your sizes"; she reorders without measuring again | Fit memory | Repeat rate |

**Where the journey can break, and the fix:** no measurements on the page (panel 3) → add them to all 13 products. Her size not stocked (panel 5) → the supplier L–3XL gate. Slow WhatsApp reply (panel 5) → quick replies and a stated response time.
