# Facts used in the film

The film describes the general Indian market a women's co-ord and saree D2C brand works in. It makes no claims about AALAYAM's own sales, prices, sourcing or policies. Please check these points against your own knowledge of the business before you publish.

| Factor | Claim on screen / in VO | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Political | Government support for handloom and trade policy shape sourcing | India's Ministry of Textiles runs handloom schemes (e.g. National Handloom Development Programme); import/export duties affect fabric costs |
| Economic | Rising incomes; price-sensitive shoppers; tax rates on clothing affect price | GST on apparel is charged at rates that depend on the sale price |
| Social | More women working; demand for comfort-wear with Indian identity | General consumer trend; qualitative |
| Technological | Instagram, UPI, fast delivery let small brands sell nationally | UPI is India's real-time payments system; social commerce and D2C logistics are widely used |
| Environmental | Buyers ask about fabric origin; natural fibres and less waste build trust | General sustainability trend; qualitative |
| Legal | Consumer protection rules, return policies, data privacy law | Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020; Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 |
