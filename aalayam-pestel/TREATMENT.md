# AALAYAM — PESTEL on the Board

Style: Whiteboard Explainer (`whiteboard`). 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 75 s. English voice-over (Kokoro, offline) with burned-in subtitles. No storyboard stop.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The list, top to bottom.** Six letters written down the left edge, each expanded in turn, like a lecture. Clear but static, and it is the cliché of every PESTEL slide.
2. **The tug-of-war.** AALAYAM drawn in the middle; tailwinds on one side, headwinds on the other, factors sorted as they come. It's dramatic, but it pushes a verdict onto factors that are mixed.
3. **The brand at the centre of a map (chosen).** AALAYAM is a magnet in the middle of an empty board. The pen draws one line outwards for each force, and the camera rides that line to a new region of the board where the factor is drawn. After six trips, the camera pulls back to show the whole board as one map: six forces around one brand.

Why 3: PESTEL is a description of the *environment around* a business. A board that literally surrounds the brand is the idea itself, and it uses two of the medium's native moves: *the pen leads the camera* and *the full-board view*.

## 2. Logline and arc

A small fashion brand sits alone on a blank board; by the end, six forces are drawn around it and it knows what it is designing for.

- **Setup**: a single magnet labelled AALAYAM on a white board. "What shapes the future of a fashion brand?"
- **Development**: six journeys out from the centre: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal.
- **Turn**: a near-silence, then a whip back to the centre and a pull-back to the full board. The six regions read as one picture.
- **Ending**: the brand's name is circled twice in the accent colour, and the pen caps itself.

## 3. Benchmark

- **RSA Animate–style continuous boards.** Learn: one board, travelling camera, a final full-board reveal. Don't take: hands, drawings, character designs.
- **Strategy-consultancy whiteboard sessions.** Learn: one letter = one region, terse labels, arrows back to the subject. Don't take: logos or slide templates.

## 4. Board layout (8000 × 4500 board units)

```
   P (1350,1050)        E (4000,800)         S (6650,1050)
                       [ AALAYAM ]  (4000,2250)
   L (1350,3450)        E (4000,3700)        T (6650,3450)
```
Order of visits: P → E → S → T → E(nv) → L, clockwise from top-left, so every ride is a short, different direction.

Colours (three markers, fixed meaning):
- navy `#1f2a44`: drawings, labels, structure;
- teal `#1f8a8a`: the PESTEL letters and the lines/arrows that connect forces to the brand (the "process");
- red `#c8363a`: the one accent per region (the word that matters most) and the AALAYAM magnet.

## 7. Cue map

96 bpm (beat 0.625 s, bar 2.5 s), C major pentatonic, original figure.
- **Intro**: a single kalimba figure (2 bars, repeats).
- **Each factor adds one instrument** ("adding one instrument per step"): P + soft woodblock pulse, E + marimba bass, S + pizzicato-like pluck counter-line, T + glockenspiel pings, E(nv) + airy pad, L + light shaker.
- **Turn**: everything stops on a downbeat before the whip (near-silence, wall-clock tick only); the first sound after the silence is the whip + magnet thunk.
- **Finale**: full ensemble returns for the pull-back, then thins to kalimba alone for the closing circle; last note lands with the pen cap click.
- Section starts land on bar lines; every new region's first stroke lands on a downbeat.

## 8. Sound design

| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | quiet room tone | magnet clack + board thump; felt-on-gloss strokes | kalimba alone |
| Factors | room tone | every stroke (hiss + body, squeak on long lines); pen hops; cap off at first factor | one instrument added per factor |
| Turn | room tone + wall clock tick | whip whoosh | silence |
| Full board | room tone | soft whoosh on pull-back | full ensemble |
| Ending | room tone | two marker circles, pen cap click | kalimba, final note |

Voice on top, music ~10 dB under and ducked further under speech; foley ~11 dB under voice; master ≈ −14 LUFS.

## 9. Type and subtitles

- Board text: single-line hand in the library's engine font, written stroke by stroke; region headings ~130 px cap height, labels ~50–60 px.
- Subtitles: off-white rounded label, Architects Daughter ~44 px, navy text, short red marker dash; bottom 12 % of every shot kept free.
- Each label finishes writing on the word that names it.

## 10. Content and facts

The six factors describe the general Indian market a women's co-ord and saree D2C brand works in. They are qualitative, well-known conditions, not figures about AALAYAM itself (no sales, prices or claims about the company). See `FACTS.md`.
