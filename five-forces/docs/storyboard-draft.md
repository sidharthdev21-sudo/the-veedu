# Porter's Five Forces: How We Analysed the Competition
## Aalayam by Anamika · Part 2 of the Business Research Explainer Series
### Storyboard and narration (draft for review)

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, English narration (the same voice as Part 1), subtle instrumental music. **Target length:** about 3 min 50 s.

**Continuity with Part 1** (`pestel-explainer`, the beige-and-rose explainer): the same palette (ivory, beige, dark brown, muted rose, white), DM Serif Display headlines with Poppins body text, the same animated character in a co-ord set, white cards, rounded chips, the same slide-up transitions, and the same top-right step tracker. Part 1 used a three-step tracker (*What it means → How it affects Aalayam → What to consider*). Here each force gets a six-step one: *Meaning → Investigated → Sources → Observed → Why it matters → Action*. A small "Part 2" tag sits in the corner throughout.

**Honesty rules built into the video**
- Every source card carries a badge. **Used** is on Google Search, Myntra and AJIO, Amazon India, Instagram, Google Trends, and Shopify and marketplaces. **Suggested** is on IndiaMART, which was not used.
- The primary research is marked **Conducted**: customer interviews and surveys, supplier quotes and discussions, and competitor product observation.
- No competitor names, rankings, market shares, prices or percentages appear anywhere. Competitor products are drawn as unnamed generic cards ("Brand A", "Brand B"…).
- Platform names appear as plain text with generic icons (a shopping bag, a phone, a search glass), never their logos.
- The "What we observed" step only states general, structural observations, such as many similar products being listed side by side. **⚠ See the review question at the end:** if your interviews and supplier discussions produced specific findings, send them and I'll use those instead.
- Scene 5 keeps the banner **Recommendations: not current capabilities** on screen throughout.
