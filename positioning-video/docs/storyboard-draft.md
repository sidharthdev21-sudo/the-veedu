# Who Aalayam Competes With: Four Levels of Competition & Perceptual Mapping
## Aalayam by Anamika · Part 3 of the Business Research Series
### Storyboard and narration (draft for review)

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, English narration (the same voice as the earlier parts), subtle instrumental music. **Target length:** about 3 min 50 s.

**Look: the dark research style of Part 1** ("How we conducted a PESTEL analysis"). Deep Obsidian background with a faint dot grid and slow glows, Charcoal cards, Soft White and Cool Grey text, Space Grotesk headlines with Inter body text. Electric Cyan is the main accent, Soft Violet the second, Neon Lime for highlights, Success Green for ticks. The same glowing connector lines, icon badges and slide-up transitions are reused from that project.

**Source material:** the published Part 3 analysis (*Aalayam Competitive Landscape*). Nothing new is claimed in the video that isn't in that analysis.

**Honesty rules built into the video**
- **Evidence chips:** a small label appears beside claims, matching the analysis page. **Fact** is green, **Observed** cyan, **Interpretation** grey, **Hypothesis** violet dashed, **Recommendation** lime.
- **Prices** are shown as observed listings ("listing seen 24 Nov 2026"), never as full price ranges or market data. Brands without a confirmed price (Bunaai, Mulmul) stay off the price map, and the narration says so.
- **Aalayam's own dot** is always hollow and labelled **Proposed**, because no public listings were found.
- **Both perceptual maps** carry a persistent banner: **Initial hypothesis: not measured customer perception**.
- **Brand names** appear as text, never logos.
