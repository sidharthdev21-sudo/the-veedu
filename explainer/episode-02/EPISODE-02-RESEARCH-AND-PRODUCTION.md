## 3. Four-level competition

| Level | Who | Why they compete |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct** (same need, similar offer) | Patient-positioned stays: Greenview Royal Stay, Casa Allay, hospital-named budget hotels (Super Hotel O "Near Aster Medicity"), Airbnb hosts marketing to bystanders | They target the same caregiver at the same hospitals. **No platform** dedicated to curating medical stays across Kochi hospitals was found in this pass; competition is property-by-property. *(Limitation: search-based; may have missed one.)* |
| **Indirect** (different solution, same need) | Booking.com / MakeMyTrip / Goibibo / Trip.com landmark pages; Airbnb; Amrita's on-campus guest house; Aster's international desk | They already answer "where do I stay near this hospital?", the general platforms by distance sorting and the hospitals by referral. |
| **Substitutes** (non-booking alternatives) | Bystander place in the patient's room; staying with relatives; commuting from home; informal lets | Each removes the need to book at all, for some families. |
| **Potential future** | OTAs adding hospital-specific filters or "attendant-friendly" tags; hospital groups expanding guest houses or partner-hotel programmes; medical-travel facilitators (e.g., Vaidam, Lyfboat) bundling stays | **Analysis.** Each already owns part of the journey: OTAs own search, hospitals own the patient, facilitators own international patients. |

| Segment | Needs | Pain points (evidence) | Current alternatives | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. | C. International patients and attendants | Arranged from abroad; trust; airport and hospital coordination | Desk-arranged, details offline [S3] | Hospital international desks, facilitators | Lower for an independent platform (hospitals already own this relationship) |
| D. Short-visit patients from other districts | One or two nights; low cost; near the OPD | Price and value; general platforms already serve them | OTAs, budget hotels | Lower (least differentiated need) |

### Persona (hypothesis, not a real person)

> **"The caregiver who isn't in the room"**: a composite built from desk research. **Not an interview.**
> - **Situation:** an adult child travelling from another Kerala district with a parent admitted to a Kochi hospital for one to three weeks, with a sibling or spouse along.
> - **Constraint:** the hospital room takes one bystander, so the others need a bed nearby, and the room has to be vacated during surgery.
> - **Decides on:** real distance to the hospital entrance, total price with every person included, a clean place they can sleep in for weeks, and food nearby.
> - **Today:** asks the hospital desk, scrolls "hotels near Amrita" pages, messages hosts on WhatsApp, or relies on relatives.
> - **To validate:** whether this person exists in meaningful numbers, how they search, and whether they'd pay for verified information. All of this needs interviews.

## 7. Strongest insight and the question it raises

**Insight (Analysis, from §2, §4b and §5):** for people visiting a hospital, **distance is the reason they choose a stay, and it's the fact listings state least consistently**. The same is true of the total price once extra people are added. Medical-stay supply already exists; *verified* medical-stay information does not, in this sample.

**Question for The Veedu:** *Can it verify the facts caregivers decide on (distance to the hospital entrance, all-in price for the whole family, long-stay fit) well enough that they'd trust it over a listing that already says "near Amrita Hospital"?*
