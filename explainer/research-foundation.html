Kerala-focused accommodation discovery · starting with medical travel to Kochi
01 · Product overview

Find a stay that fits a hospital visit — not a holiday.

What

Accommodation discovery

A Kerala-focused platform for finding stays — homestays, serviced apartments, guest houses.

Who, first

Medical travellers to Kochi

Patients from other districts and states, and the caregivers and families who accompany them.

Where it stands

Working prototype

Listings, map, host dashboard and reviews exist. No proven demand, bookings or revenue yet.

So this is treated as a pre-launch concept — the research comes before the roadmap.

02 · Problem hypothesis
Hypothesis · not yet tested

Existing accommodation platforms may not adequately address the specific needs of medical travellers and their families.

It holds if we find…

  • Caregivers struggle to judge distance, access and total cost
  • Long or uncertain stays don't fit nightly-booking platforms
  • Families fall back on referrals and word of mouth

It breaks if we find…

  • Hospitals already arrange stays that families are happy with
  • Booking apps or OTAs already meet these needs well enough
  • The pain is real but rare, or nobody will pay to fix it
Research standard

Every claim gets a label.

Fact

Verifiable, with a source URL and a publication date.

Assumption

Believed true and acted on, but not yet checked.

Hypothesis

A testable claim, with a defined way to prove it wrong.

Recommendation

A decision that follows from the evidence.

No invented interviews, reviews, statistics or competitor details. Gaps get reported as limitations.

04 · Initial geography hypothesis

Start with one cluster, not all of Kerala.

Hypothesis

Edappally–Cheranalloor first

Two major hospitals in one corridor, and listings that already use them as landmarks.

Why one cluster?

Small enough to verify every property in person, and to build relationships with hospital coordinators.

Comparison clusters

Rajagiri (Aluva) and Lakeshore (Maradu) — to check whether the pattern repeats.

08 · Evidence collection plan

Five sources, each with a known blind spot.

Government

Tourism & health reports

Kerala Tourism, Ministry of Tourism, state budgets.

Blind spot: national scale, not accommodation-specific.

Hospitals

Official websites

International-patient desks, guest-house options, services offered.

Blind spot: marketing copy, not real usage.

Listings

Accommodation supply

Airbnb, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, OYO, Kerala Tourism directory.

Blind spot: what hosts claim, not what guests found.

Reviews

Guest feedback

Google Maps and OTA reviews near target hospitals, theme-coded.

Blind spot: rarely says whether the guest was a medical traveller.

Primary

Interviews

Caregivers, patients, hospital coordinators and hosts.

Blind spot: small sample; recruitment bias.
