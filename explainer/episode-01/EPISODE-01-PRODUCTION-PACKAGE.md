# The Veedu: Episode 01 production package

**Series:** The Veedu, a Product Management case study
**Episode:** 01, "Not everyone in Kochi is on holiday"
**Runtime:** about 84 seconds · 16:9 · 24 fps cinematic · dark interface
**Purpose:** set up a serious product investigation. This episode does not present findings.

---

## 1. Video title

**Primary:** *Not Everyone in Kochi Is on Holiday*
**Subtitle card:** The Veedu · Episode 01: Framing the Problem

Alternatives:
- *A Stay Near the Hospital: Starting The Veedu*
- *Before I Build: The Veedu, Episode 01*

---

## 2. Core narrative

> Some people travel to Kochi for a hospital, not a holiday. I suspect that finding somewhere to stay works differently for them and their families, and that general booking platforms may not handle it well. Kochi's tourism data shows why it's a reasonable place to start. It also shows what the data can't tell me: how many medical travellers there are and what they struggle with. Episode 01 is about naming that gap honestly, before building anything.

**Emotional arc:** quiet curiosity, then a clear hypothesis, then grounding in real data, then an honest admission of what's unknown, then a sense of purpose.
**Tone:** a Product Manager thinking out loud, not an advert. No hype words ("revolutionary", "disrupt", "seamless").

**Voice direction:** a natural, unhurried male or female voice. Warm, confident and thoughtful, like someone explaining their own project to a respected colleague. Use Indian-English or neutral international English. Aim for about 140 words a minute, with real pauses at the "//" marks.

## 5. AI video generation prompts (one per scene)

**How to use these.** The prompts below suit text-to-video tools such as Runway, Veo, Kling, Sora and Pika. Generate the footage only, with **no on-screen text**: AI models render text poorly. Add all text, stat tiles and maps in your editor (After Effects, Premiere, DaVinci or Canva) using Sections 6 and 7. Scenes 5–7 are motion graphics, so their prompts are design specs for the editor rather than for an AI model.

**Shared style suffix.** Append this to every AI prompt:
> *cinematic, 16:9, 24fps, anamorphic look, shallow depth of field, low-key lighting, deep blue-black shadows (#08090C), subtle cool cyan rim light (#6CF0FF), restrained colour grade, soft film grain, no text, no logos, no signage, no recognisable faces, documentary realism, calm and respectful mood*

**Scene 1: Cold open (AI)**
> Blue-hour wide shot of the Fort Kochi waterfront in Kerala, India. Traditional cantilevered Chinese fishing nets stand as dark silhouettes against a deep indigo sky with the last trace of dusk light on the horizon. Calm water reflects faint cyan light. The camera does a slow, steady dolly-in. Gentle mist, no people in focus, quiet and contemplative. + style suffix

**Scene 2: Product intro (AI plate)**
> Exterior of a modern hospital forecourt in a South Indian city at dusk. Warm, softly lit windows and a covered drop-off area. A family of two adults walks slowly away from camera carrying an overnight bag, out of focus. Tropical plants, light rain-wet pavement reflecting light. Slow rack focus from foreground foliage to building. No signage, no brand names, no identifiable faces. + style suffix
> *Editor:* hold 3 s, then crossfade to the UI card on the Charcoal `#0E1015` panel.

**Scene 3: Why me (AI)**
> Night interior, over-the-shoulder view of a wooden desk in a Kerala home. An open laptop shows soft grey wireframe layouts on a dark screen, slightly out of focus. A ring of house keys rests beside a notebook. A warm desk lamp is the only practical light, and a cool cyan glow comes from the screen. Slight handheld drift, intimate and thoughtful. Hands only, no face. + style suffix

**Scene 4: Hypothesis (AI, left half of frame)**
> Medium shot of an adult caregiver seen from behind in silhouette, standing at a window in a modest rented room at night, holding a phone with a dim glow. Outside the window, blurred city lights and the distant lit windows of a hospital building. Warm lamp light inside, cool blue outside. Very slow push-in. Quiet, tired, hopeful mood, dignified and not dramatic. No visible face, no medical equipment, no distress. + style suffix
> *Editor:* place the footage in the left 55% of the frame and the hypothesis card on the right.

**Scene 5: Why Kochi (motion graphics spec)**
> Dark map of Kerala built from an official district-boundary vector (use a public-domain or government boundary file). The outline is drawn in Cool Grey `#A3A5B0` at 1 px. Ernakulam fills Electric Cyan `#6CF0FF` at 35% with a 2 px cyan stroke. To the right, a Charcoal `#0E1015` stat tile shows "2.5 crore" in Neon Lime `#D4FF6A`. Map draw-on takes 1.0 s; the number counts up over 1.2 s with an ease-out.
> *Optional AI B-roll (0.5 s cutaway):* an aerial drone shot over Kochi city and backwaters at dusk, with high-rises beside palm-lined water channels and a slow lateral glide. + style suffix

**Scene 6: Medical data and caveat (motion graphics spec)**
> Two Charcoal tiles side by side on the Obsidian background. The left tile reads "6.44 lakh" in Neon Lime, labelled *foreign arrivals for medical treatment · India · 2024*. The right tile reads *Tourist visits*, in Cool Grey, with no number. A large "≠" in Soft Violet `#B48CFF` wipes in between them. Fade-ups are staggered by 0.3 s. No AI footage.

**Scene 7: The gap (motion graphics spec)**
> Continue from the Scene 5 map. Zoom 4× into Ernakulam toward a small cluster marker; label it "Kochi hospital cluster" and add a "schematic" tag. A dashed circle in Soft Violet draws on over 0.8 s, then a large thin "?" fades in at the centre. Cool Grey label: "Data gap". Keep it slow and still.

**Scene 8: Research agenda and close (motion graphics, then AI)**
> Motion graphics: four Charcoal question cards in a 2×2 grid, each with a thin Electric Cyan top border, rising 24 px and fading in with a 0.15 s stagger.
> AI footage for the final 4 s: *First light over the Kochi backwaters. A still channel lined with coconut palms, mist on the water, and a single small boat drifting slowly across the frame. The camera rises slowly. Hopeful, calm, beginning-of-a-journey mood.* + style suffix

**Negative prompt for every AI scene:**
> text, captions, watermark, logo, hospital name, brand signage, identifiable faces, patients in beds, medical procedures, blood, ambulance sirens, distress, crowds, oversaturated colours, neon overload, cartoon, 3D render look

## 7. Suggested sound design

| Layer | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Music bed** | Minimal ambient piano or felt-piano over a soft analogue synth pad, in a major key with suspended chords, around 70 BPM. Use a royalty-free or licensed track. Keep it at −22 to −26 LUFS under the VO and duck 4–6 dB during speech. |
| **Kerala texture** | Scene 1: gentle water lapping and very distant evening birds. Scene 8: dawn birdsong and soft water. Keep it subtle so it doesn't become a travel-ad cliché. |
| **UI sounds** | Soft, low-pitched "tick" on each card or pill reveal; a muted, airy "whoosh" (−30 dB) on map draws; a gentle digital "count" texture under the number count-ups, ending on a soft click. |
| **Scene 6 moment** | Drop the music to near silence for about 1 s when "≠" appears, then bring the pad back. This marks the honest caveat. |
| **Scene 7 gap** | Low sustained tone with no melody, a held breath. |
| **Ending** | The music resolves on a single sustained chord under "Let's find out." Leave 1.5 s of room tone on the end card. |
| **Avoid** | Hospital sounds (beeps, sirens), dramatic risers, cinematic "braaam" hits, stock corporate ukulele. |

**VO mix:** −16 LUFS integrated, light compression, de-essed, with a touch of room so it doesn't sound studio-sterile.

## 8. Transitions

| From → To | Transition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 → 2 | Slow cross-dissolve (12 frames) from waterfront to hospital forecourt | Holiday Kochi becomes hospital Kochi, which is the episode's premise |
| 2 (footage → UI) | The footage darkens to Obsidian and the UI card fades up from the dark | Introduces the product layer |
| 2 → 3 | Match cut from the UI card to the laptop screen in the desk shot | The product links to the person building it |
| 3 → 4 | Hard cut on the pause after "differently?" | Lands the question before the answer |
| 4 → 5 | The hypothesis card slides left and becomes the map panel (shared Charcoal panel) | Moves from idea to evidence |
| 5 → 6 | The stat tile morphs in place: the "2.5 crore" tile becomes the "6.44 lakh" tile | Same shape, different statistic, which visually supports the "≠" point |
| 6 → 7 | Zoom from the national tile back into the Kochi map | Moves from national data to the local unknown |
| 7 → 8 | The "?" scales down and becomes the first question card | The gap becomes the research agenda |
| 8 → End | Cross-dissolve to backwaters at first light, then fade to black on the end card | A beginning, not a conclusion |

Motion principle: use no more than one transition style per scene change and ease-out curves throughout. No spins, glitches or flashes.

### Research limitations to carry into Episode 02
1. **No official count of medical travellers to Kerala or Kochi was found.** The national figure covers foreign arrivals only and is not broken down by state. Domestic medical travellers from other districts and states, likely the core segment, are not counted in any source reviewed.
2. **Tourist visits are not medical travellers.** Kerala's visit statistics count all visits. They are used here only to show that Ernakulam is the state's busiest district, never as a proxy for demand.
3. **Ernakulam's 2024 and 2025 district values** need to be checked against the PDF tables before being shown on screen.
4. **The Kerala Economic Review and the Ministry of Tourism dashboard** have not yet been read in full. Review both for healthcare infrastructure and any health-tourism data.
5. **No customer interviews have been conducted.** Every statement about caregiver needs in this episode is a hypothesis and is labelled as one.
