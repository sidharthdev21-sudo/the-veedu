# Pilot kit: research guides

Templates for Weeks 1–2. Use them as written, then record what actually happened in the CSV sheets in this folder. **Don't paraphrase answers into quotes, and don't share any participant detail without consent.**

---

## A. Consent script (read before every session)

> "Thanks for helping. I'm researching how families find accommodation when someone is being treated at a hospital in Kochi. I'm building a small website called The Veedu, and I want to learn from your experience. There are no right answers. I'd like to take notes [and record audio, if you agree]. I won't use your name, or anything that identifies you or your family member, and I won't ask about the medical condition. You can stop at any time. Is that okay?"

Record: participant ID, consent given (y/n), recording consent (y/n), date.

---

## B. Caregiver interview (Week 1 · 20–30 min · past behaviour only)

**Screener:** arranged accommodation in Kochi for a family member's hospital treatment in the last 12 months, or is arranging one now. Note stay length, party size and where they travelled from. **Don't ask about diagnosis.**

1. Tell me about the last time you needed somewhere to stay for a hospital visit in Kochi. Where were you travelling from, and who came with you?
2. How did you find the accommodation? Which websites, apps, contacts or recommendations did you use?
3. How did you decide whether a place was close enough to the hospital?
4. What information was missing before you contacted the host or property?
5. How did you check availability and confirm the booking?
6. What made you trust a property, or reject one?
7. Did the hospital help with accommodation? What happened?
8. What was the most stressful or time-consuming part?
9. If you stayed more than a few nights, what mattered that wouldn't matter on a short trip?
10. What did it cost in the end, compared with what you expected?

**Probe for specifics** ("what did you do next?", "can you show me the message?"), not opinions ("would you use…?").

## C. Host interview (Week 1 · 20 min)

1. How do guests find you today? Which channels bring the most useful enquiries?
2. How often do guests mention a hospital visit? Roughly what share?
3. How do you keep availability up to date across channels?
4. What makes an enquiry easy or hard to respond to?
5. What happens with cancellations or changes on longer stays?
6. Would you confirm your property details (distance, price including extra guests, availability) with me in person? What would make that worth your time?
7. How would you feel about receiving enquiries directly on WhatsApp from The Veedu?

## D. Synthesis (end of Week 1)

- Write one observation per sticky, tagged with the participant ID (P-01 caregiver, H-01 host).
- Cluster the stickies into themes, and map each theme to H1–H4.
- For each hypothesis, record **supports / contradicts / unclear**, with the count of participants behind it. **Don't generalise from one person.**

---

## E. Usability test script (Week 2 · 30 min · live website)

**Before Week 2 (pre-flight, found on 28 Nov 2026):** the live site had **0 published listings**, and its location picker listed **12 areas but no hospitals**. Before testing:
1. add Aster Medcity (and later Amrita) as search points;
2. publish at least 3–5 **host-confirmed** properties near Aster Medcity.

Without these, Tasks A–C can't be completed, and failures would reflect missing supply rather than the design.

**Setup:** the participant's own phone if possible; think aloud; the observer doesn't help. If the participant isn't a recent caregiver, label them a **proxy** in the sheet.

| Task | Prompt to read aloud |
|---|---|
| A | "You need to stay near Aster Medcity for five nights with two family members. Find a suitable place to stay." |
| B | "Compare two properties and tell me which one you would shortlist and why." |
| C | "Check whether the property is ready to stay in, and work out how you would contact the host." |
| D | "What information would you still need before you'd book?" |

**After the tasks:**
- "In your own words, what does 'verified' mean on this site?"
- "Compared with how you searched last time, did this make you more or less sure? Why?"
- Confidence to contact the host: 1–5.

**Don't count** a click on WhatsApp as booking intent. Do **not** send real enquiries to hosts during usability sessions.
