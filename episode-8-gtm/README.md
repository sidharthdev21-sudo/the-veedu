# Episode 8 · Go-to-Market Strategy & Recording Data

A simple 2 min 24 s explainer (1920 × 1080, 30 fps) in the short-version style. Part 1: the go-to-market strategy (what we sell, who to, how we reach her, launch in three steps), from `../marketing-plan/`. Part 2: how to record data (five tabs, six Instagram numbers per post, an enquiry log without personal data, tracking tags, a weekly 30-minute routine) and the three numbers to watch.

Render: `npm install`, `python scripts/build_audio.py` (rebuilds the audio mix, which isn't committed), then `npx remotion render Episode8GTM out/aalayam-episode-8-gtm.mp4`. Spreadsheet templates for the recording system are in `../episode-7/data/`.
