## What is go-to-market?  (0:00)

A great product nobody hears about doesn't sell. A go-to-market strategy fixes that.
It answers three simple questions. What are we selling? Who are we selling to? And how will we reach them?

## What we sell  (0:11)

What are we selling? Comfortable cotton co-ord sets, in sizes L to triple XL, priced from one thousand to seventeen hundred rupees.
And one clear promise: clothes that fit the first time. Real measurements on every product, honest prices, and size help on WhatsApp.

## Who we sell to  (0:28)

Who are we selling to? Not everyone. Women aged twenty-five to forty who wear L to triple XL, in Kerala, and Malayali women in India's big cities.
Picture Anu. She's thirty-two, wears an XL top and a double XL pant, and has sent back clothes that didn't fit. She trusts real women more than models.

## How we reach her  (0:48)

How will we reach her? Through the places she already is.
Instagram Reels showing real women in every size. A WhatsApp size check. Our own online store. Google, for people searching nearby. Small creators who wear our sizes. And try-on days in Kochi.

## Launch in three steps  (1:05)

We won't launch big. We'll launch small, and learn.
Step one: get noticed. Step two: win a first order. Step three: bring her back for a second.
We start with just six styles, each in every size from L to triple XL, and grow what works.

## Why record data  (1:19)

But a plan only works if we can see what's working. And last time, the results weren't written down.
So from now on, we record five things: every post, every enquiry, every order, every product, and every supplier. All in one shared sheet, with five tabs.

## How to record  (1:35)

Recording is simple.
After each post, note six numbers from Instagram: reach, likes, saves, shares, profile visits, and link clicks.
For every message, note the date, the channel, what she asked about, her size, and whether she ordered. No names, no phone numbers.
Put a tracking tag on every link, so we know which post brought each visit.
And every Monday, spend thirty minutes updating the sheet.

## From records to decisions  (1:58)

Then we watch three numbers. How many people save our posts. How many size checks turn into orders. And how many orders come back for a different size.
One rule: don't judge too early. Wait about four weeks, and a few hundred visits, before deciding.

## Next 90 days  (2:13)

So, the next ninety days: fix the basics, launch small, measure and decide.
Know who you're for. Reach her where she is. Write it all down. Then decide.
