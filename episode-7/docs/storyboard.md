| 7 | 4:30–4:58 | Connecting the business | Seven-node operating loop with a travelling light; then the five-table data model linked by content_id, UTM and SKU; three "without the loop" warnings. | These activities only become a business when they're connected. One data model links them: content, enquiries, store sessions, products and suppliers, joined by content IDs, UTM links and the SKU. Without that loop, a brand designs without demand, sources without margins, and launches without learning. |
