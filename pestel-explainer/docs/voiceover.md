# Voiceover script

Voice: Kokoro (af_heart, US English), speed 1.05. Total length 2:58.

## Introduction  (0:00)

Meet Aalayam by Anamika, an Indian clothing brand that makes co-ords and sarees for modern women.
Like every business, it's shaped by changes happening outside the brand.
PESTEL is a simple way to look at those outside forces.
It stands for Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental and Legal. Let's take them one at a time.

## Political  (0:22)

Political factors are about government decisions.
For a clothing brand, that means G S T on garments, rules for the textile industry, and schemes like Make in India that support local manufacturing.
A change in tax can change what an outfit costs the customer.
So Aalayam should keep an eye on new policies, and make the most of support for Indian-made textiles.

## Economic  (0:46)

Economic factors are about money: how much things cost, and how much people can spend.
When inflation rises, fabric gets more expensive, and shoppers think twice before buying.
And with so many brands competing online, price really matters.
Aalayam should price carefully, plan for rising fabric costs, and show customers clear value.

## Social  (1:07)

Social factors are about people: how they live, what they like, and how they shop.
Today, more women want clothes that look good and feel comfortable, from workdays to weekends.
They discover fashion on Instagram, trust real reviews, and happily shop on their phones.
So Aalayam should design for comfort, follow trends, and stay active where its customers spend time.

## Technological  (1:30)

Technological factors are about the tools that help a business grow.
An online store lets Aalayam sell across India, and social commerce turns a post into a purchase.
A I recommendations can suggest the right outfit, and simple analytics show what customers love.
The brand should build a smooth shopping experience, and use its data to make smarter choices.

## Environmental  (1:52)

Environmental factors are about our planet.
Fashion creates a lot of textile waste, and more shoppers now ask how their clothes are made.
Sustainable fabrics, less waste, and eco-friendly packaging all make a difference.
Small steps like natural fabrics and recyclable packaging can build trust and set Aalayam apart.

## Legal  (2:13)

Legal factors are the rules every business has to follow.
Selling online means following consumer protection and e-commerce rules, like clear prices and fair returns.
Every garment needs a proper label, G S T must be filed correctly, and customer data has to be kept safe.
Getting this right protects Aalayam, and earns its customers' confidence.

## Conclusion  (2:35)

Put all six together, and you get a clear picture of the world around the brand.
Rules, money, people, technology and the planet all shape what the brand can do.
By watching these forces, Aalayam by Anamika can make better decisions and spot new opportunities early.
That's PESTEL: six simple questions about the world outside your business.
