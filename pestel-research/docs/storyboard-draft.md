# How We Conducted a PESTEL Analysis: Aalayam by Anamika
## Storyboard and narration (draft for review)

**Format:** 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, English narration, subtle instrumental music. **Target length:** about 3 min 45 s.
**Look:** dark, minimal, "research dashboard" style. Deep Obsidian `#08090C` background, Charcoal `#0E1015` cards, Soft White `#ECECF1` text, Cool Grey `#A3A5B0` secondary text. Electric Cyan `#6CF0FF` is the main accent, Soft Violet `#B48CFF` the second accent, Neon Lime `#D4FF6A` for highlights, and Success Green `#5EE6A8` for ticks and "done" states. Thin line icons, rounded cards, a glowing connector line that carries the story from scene to scene. No stock imagery.

**Through-line:** one research "project board" that fills up as the video goes. Each scene adds to it, so by the end the viewer has watched the whole analysis being built, from a blank board to finished insights.

**Honesty rules built into the video**
- Every tool in the toolkit is marked **Used**, as you confirmed. A separate **Recommended next** group shows customer interviews and Aalayam's own sales data, which were not part of this research.
- The findings are general conditions for Indian women's fashion brands, not figures about Aalayam. There are no market-size numbers or statistics.
- Scene 5 is labelled **Potential recommendations: not current capabilities** on screen the whole time.

## Facts the narration relies on (to confirm before the final render)

| Claim | What it rests on |
|---|---|
| Government supports Indian textile manufacturing | Make in India; textile schemes such as PM MITRA parks and the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for textiles |
| GST on a garment depends on its price | GST on apparel is charged at different rates depending on the sale value per piece |
| India has banned many single-use plastics | Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2021 (ban in force from July 2022) |
| Online sellers must show clear prices and return policies | Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 |
| Products must be labelled properly | Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 |
| Customer data must be protected | Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 |

Social, economic and technological findings are stated as general trends without numbers, so they don't depend on a single statistic.
