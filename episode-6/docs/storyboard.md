| 9 | 3:56–4:29 | ERRC grid | A 2×2 ERRC grid filling quadrant by quadrant, then the value-proposition transformation: the old claim is struck through, and "Comfort co-ords that fit the first time" appears. | The ERRC grid makes it concrete. Eliminate the inflated M R P, and the rule that a set comes in one size. Reduce the range to six to eight core silhouettes, and rely less on marketplaces. Raise fit information, with garment measurements, model size and daylight photos. Make one size exchange free. Create split sizing with an adjustable waist and two lengths, plus a WhatsApp fit check that remembers each customer's sizes. The promise shifts from comfortable, stylish and accessible, which everyone claims, to comfort co-ords that fit the first time. |
